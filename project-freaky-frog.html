Unity Project

Freaky Frog

A 3D survival game where the frog collects flies to score points while being chased by a predatory bird.

Game

Project Overview

The goal is simple: collect as many flies as possible before the bird catches the frog.

Key Features

  • Frog movement and jumping across the environment
  • Fly collection and real-time score tracking
  • Predatory bird navigation and pursuit
  • Animated frog movement and jumping
  • Sound effects for jumping, collection and the bird
  • Live score display during gameplay

Technical Implementation

Engine: Unity
Language: C#
Focus: Physics, animation, AI navigation, audio, gameplay systems

The frog’s movement uses physics and Quaternion-based rotation to control jumping in different directions while keeping orientation aligned with movement. The Animator system handles movement and jump animations alongside gameplay movement.

The bird uses Unity’s NavMesh system for pathfinding. Navigation meshes were baked into the environment, allowing the bird to find paths through the scene and pursue the frog.

An Audio Mixer balances gameplay sounds, including the bird, frog movement and fly collection, so no individual sound dominates the experience.

Challenges & Solutions

Balancing multiple gameplay sounds: Unity’s Audio Mixer was used to manage and balance the different sound sources.

Directional frog movement: Physics, Quaternion rotation and the Animator system were combined to make the frog jump naturally in different directions while maintaining correct orientation.

Performance & Optimization

Gameplay movement, navigation, animation and audio were coordinated to keep the experience responsive during play.
