Unity Project

Freaky Frog

A 3D survival game where the frog collects flies to score points while being chased by a predatory bird.

Game

Project Overview

The goal is simple: collect as many flies as possible before the bird catches the frog.

Key Features

  • Frog movement and jumping across the environment
  • Fly collection and real-time score tracking
  • Predatory bird navigation and pursuit
  • Animated frog movement and jumping
  • Sound effects for jumping, collection and the bird
  • Live score display during gameplay

Technical Implementation

Engine: Unity
Language: C#
Focus: Physics, animation, AI navigation, audio, gameplay systems

Balancing multiple gameplay sounds

Unity’s Audio Mixer was used to manage and balance the different sound sources.

Directional frog movement

Physics, Quaternion rotation and the Animator system were combined to make the frog jump naturally in different directions while maintaining correct orientation.

Performance & Optimization

Gameplay movement, navigation, animation and audio were coordinated to keep the experience responsive during play.
