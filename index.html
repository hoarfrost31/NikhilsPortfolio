About Me

Nikhil Kapse

Computer Science Graduate · Unity & XR Developer · Technical Educator

I’m a Computer Science graduate, Unity & XR Developer, and technical educator specializing in Unity, C#, AR/VR, and interactive 3D development.

My career began in software and VR development, where I worked hands-on with Unity and C# to build interactive experiences. I later moved into technical education and built a full-time career teaching Unity and immersive technologies.

Through Udemy, I have taught 2500+ learners worldwide through practical, project-based courses focused on Unity and development. I have also worked as Visiting Faculty in AR/VR, teaching immersive technology and Unity-based development in an academic environment.

This experience gave me the opportunity to go deeply into both the technology and the ability to communicate complex technical concepts clearly.

I am now transitioning back into full-time hands-on software development, bringing together my development experience, Computer Science foundation, and years of deep technical specialization in Unity and XR.

Core Areas

  • XR/VR
  • OpenXR
  • Meta Quest / Standalone VR
  • Git & Software Development Practices
