Unity Project

Archery Rush

A time-based archery game where the player aims at moving targets and tries to score as many points as possible before the timer runs out.

Game

Project Overview

The targets move through the environment, requiring the player to continuously adjust their aim and timing.

Key Features

  • Mouse-controlled archery bow movement
  • Physics-based bow interaction and aiming
  • Moving targets with animated motion
  • Score-based gameplay with a time limit
  • Rain particle effects creating a dynamic atmosphere

Technical Implementation

Engine: Unity
Language: C#
Focus: Physics, input handling, Quaternion rotation, animation, particle systems

Bow movement and aiming

Mouse input was translated into controlled bow movement while maintaining consistent orientation through Quaternion-based rotation.

Creating an atmospheric environment

A rain particle system adds depth and a stronger sense of atmosphere during gameplay.

Performance & Optimization

Input, animated targets and particle effects were coordinated to keep the timed gameplay responsive.
